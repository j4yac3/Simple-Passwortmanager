Welcome to the JM Password Manager Setup!

WHAT YOU SHOULD KNOW BEFORE CONTINUING:

* 100% LOCAL: All passwords are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on this
  computer only. No cloud, no accounts, no tracking.

* MASTER PASSWORD: You will create a master password (minimum 12
  characters) that protects your entire vault. There is no password
  recovery without your recovery code.

* RECOVERY CODE: After the first start, the app shows you a 28-character
  recovery code. Write it down and keep it safe - it is the ONLY way to
  reset a forgotten master password.

* AUTO-LOCK: For your safety, the vault locks itself automatically after
  5 minutes of inactivity.

* CLIPBOARD: Copied passwords are removed from the clipboard
  automatically after 15 seconds.

* YOUR DATA LIVES HERE:  %USERPROFILE%\.jm-passwortmanager\vault.db
  Uninstalling this program will NOT delete your password database.
